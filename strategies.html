📖 Key Concepts for Gold Trading

Why Gold Moves

  • USD Strength — Gold and DXY are inversely correlated. Weak dollar → gold up.
  • Real Yields — When US 10Y TIPS yield drops, gold rises (lower opportunity cost of holding non-yielding asset).
  • Fed Policy — Rate cuts → gold up. Rate hikes → gold down.
  • Geopolitics — Wars, conflicts, uncertainty → gold safe-haven bid.
  • Inflation — Gold is an inflation hedge. Rising CPI → gold up.
  • Central Bank Buying — China, India, Russia accumulating gold → bullish structural trend.

Gold Scalping Tips

  • Use 5m or 1m charts for entry timing
  • Set SL at 1.5× ATR from entry (dynamic, not fixed pips)
  • Target TP1 at 1× R:R, TP2 at 2× R:R — bank TP1+TP2, let TP3 ride
  • Trade only during London + New York overlap (11:30–14:30 GMT) for max liquidity
  • Check DXY and real yields for directional bias before entering
  • Use the Aurum Engine checklist for disciplined entries — never skip the setup

Risk Management Rules

  • Never risk more than 0.5–1% of equity per trade
  • Maximum 3 concurrent positions
  • Stop loss is never widened — if hit, exit and reassess
  • Take profits at TP1 + TP2 first, then move SL to breakeven
  • No trading during high-impact news (NFP, FOMC, CPI) unless you have a specific strategy for it
