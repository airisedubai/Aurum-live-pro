Strategy Reference Guide

🧸 For Laymen: The Rubber Band

Imagine price is a rubber band attached to a moving car (the trend). If the car stops but the band stretches too far (overbought/oversold), it will eventually snap back.

Strategy: Wait for the band to stretch extreme (RSI > 70 or < 30), then trade the snap back to the car.

🌊 ICT Concepts: Liquidity Sweep

Institutions need liquidity to fill large orders. They often push price above old highs to trigger "Stop Losses" (which are buy orders) before reversing.

Entry: Wait for a sweep of a high/low, then a strong reversal candle closing back inside the range.
